# Proposed Telegram privacy addition — owner review required

Draft amendment only. This does not publish/replace the existing privacy notice.

If you connect Telegram, Wrytely uses the official Telegram Bot API to receive your
private messages and send responses or preparation results. Telegram processes those
messages and attachments under its own policies. Wrytely stores the numeric private
chat identifier, account-link generation, message/update identifiers, connection
preferences, messages you authorize for the agent, and delivery state. Your Telegram
username is not used as proof of your Wrytely account identity.

Preparation messages/files can include selected course instructions, exact excerpts,
your clarification questions and source-coverage warnings. Messages processed by the
agent may also be sent to Wrytely's configured Meta-model inference provider as
described in the existing notice. A preparation checklist is not a submitted or
completed assignment, and linking does not authorize sending data to another person
or group. This pilot supports private one-to-one chats only.

You can disable replies and proactive notifications separately, choose quiet hours,
unlink Telegram, or send /stop to pause Wrytely and disable Telegram messages.
Unlinking does not automatically delete historical agent records, recall a message
already transmitted, or resolve an uncertain delivery. No automatic retention period
or automated deletion process is promised. Contact support@wrytely.io for privacy or
deletion requests. Existing privacy/contact and retention disclosures otherwise apply.
