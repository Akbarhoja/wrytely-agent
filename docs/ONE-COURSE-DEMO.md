Use cuts/time compression for slow work and label it. Do not imply seconds-long
latency if the measured run takes minutes. Blur student identifiers, messages from
other people, grades, tokens, private URLs and course content not needed for evidence.
Use original synthetic fixtures when a real-course recording would reveal others.
Never fake a phone message, provider response or business approval.

If WhatsApp stays restricted, a scripted transport recording is explicitly simulated
and does not satisfy the current live-phone requirement. Escalate that exact blocker
to Akbar before selecting a fallback or changing the approved delivery claims.

## Capture once during the bounded acceptance run

Record privately: exact candidate SHA; migration ledger; selected connection/course;
collection completion and missing resources; actual Meta model/configured prices;
global opening liabilities and test allowance; reservation count/known/unknown usage;
monitor decisions/next check; offer and acceptance replay; artifact/check IDs and
file checksums; real phone receipt plus matched signed delivered status; revision
identity; changed-source invalidation; restart/pause; laptop-closed start/end times.

Publish only redacted evidence and synthetic fixtures. A passed model assessment
does not mean instructor approval or a factual guarantee. Saved acceptance and
provider accepted responses are not delivered evidence. Do not rerun ambiguous
inference/sends simply to get a cleaner recording.
